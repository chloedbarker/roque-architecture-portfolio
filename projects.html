Projects

A selection of academic, professional, and competition work focused on architectural design, representation, technical development, and visualization across Spain and the Netherlands.

Studio (Collaborative) · Almàssera, Spain · 2025

Medialab Almàssera

A multidisciplinary cultural center developed within a former agricultural warehouse, designed to connect the town of Almàssera with its surrounding farmland. The project integrates an auditorium, workshops, exhibition spaces, a greenhouse, and a café within a 7,000 m² site, exploring the relationship between an existing industrial structure and new architectural interventions.

Collaborative design Spatial systems AutoCAD Blender Adobe Suite

Internship · studio PROTOTYPE, Blaricum, Netherlands · 2025

Villa Blaricum

A private residence set within the natural landscape of North Holland, featuring charred timber cladding chosen for durability against humidity, insects, and fire. The project focused on facade studies and roof detailing, balancing material performance, constructability, and architectural character.

Facade & roof studies Archicad SketchUp Enscape Adobe Suite

Internship · studio PROTOTYPE, Warmond, Netherlands · 2025

Warmond Woonboot

Architectural documentation for a completed houseboat project in Warmond, developed during an internship at studio PROTOTYPE. The work involved translating the Archicad model into simplified, polished drawings for press and award submissions, with an emphasis on clarity, precision, and visual communication.

Technical documentation Archicad AutoCAD Adobe Suite

Competition · l'Horta de València, Spain · 2025

K-seta

A lightweight community structure designed for the agricultural landscape surrounding Pluto in Valencia. Functioning as both a tool shed and a social shelter, the proposal uses recycled scaffolding and permeable materials to minimize environmental impact while supporting gardening, gathering, and broader cultural activities.

Team competition Concept development Blender Adobe Suite

Studio · Benicàssim, Spain · 2024

C/ La Cort — Font

A residential development composed of six single-family homes and one café near the beach in Benicàssim. Inspired by fractal geometry and modular repetition, the project explores how repeated spatial systems can generate variation while maintaining formal coherence, encouraging both privacy and social interaction in a coastal neighborhood setting.

Design development Spatial systems AutoCAD Blender Adobe Suite

Studio · Benicàssim, Spain · 2023

Desierto de las Palmas

A home and art studio located within the ruins of a historic monastery, designed to foster contemplation and connection with nature. The project integrates living, working, and meditation spaces within a modular timber structure inspired by Scandinavian architectural precedents, creating a quiet retreat for artistic practice.

Full design development AutoCAD Blender Adobe Suite

Studio · Ciutat Vella, València, Spain · 2023

Art School & Residence

A contemporary art school and residential complex organized around courtyards to maximize natural light and ventilation. The project includes atelier spaces, exhibition areas, staff rooms, and a 14-room residence, using a double-skin facade system to filter sunlight, improve environmental performance, and create a strong relationship between interior and exterior.

Design & visualization AutoCAD Blender Adobe Suite
